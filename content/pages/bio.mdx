## Research

<p className="bio-section__intro">My research sits between human-centered AI and the technical foundations of intelligent systems. The recurring question is not only what an AI system can do, but whether people can understand, evaluate, and rely on it over time.</p>

<div className="bio-focus-grid">
  <section>
    <p className="bio-focus-grid__index">01</p>
    <h3>Explainable and responsible AI</h3>
    <p>Methods and interfaces that make model behavior easier to inspect, with particular attention to fairness, trustworthiness, and responsibility.</p>
  </section>
  <section>
    <p className="bio-focus-grid__index">02</p>
    <h3>Memory and evolving agents</h3>
    <p>Reliable long-term memory and open-ended co-evolution in language-model agents that operate across extended interactions.</p>
  </section>
  <section>
    <p className="bio-focus-grid__index">03</p>
    <h3>AI for Science and visualization</h3>
    <p>Visual and computational tools that help people reason with complex scientific data, including occasional work with marine environments.</p>
  </section>
